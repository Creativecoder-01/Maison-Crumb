Our Story

Baked With Heart, Made Fresh Every Day

Maison Crumb began with a simple love for the comforting ritual of freshly baked bread and pastries. We believe the best bakes are made with time, care, and thoughtfully chosen ingredients.

From buttery croissants and artisan breads to delicate pastries and celebration cakes, our creations are baked in small batches throughout the day to keep them fresh, flavorful, and worth slowing down for.

We use quality ingredients and seasonal touches wherever possible, creating food that feels as good as it tastes. Whether you're starting your morning with a warm pastry and coffee or sharing something sweet with the people you love, we're here to make everyday moments a little more delicious.
